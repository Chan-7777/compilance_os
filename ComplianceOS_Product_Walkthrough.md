## What is ComplianceOS?

ComplianceOS is an all-in-one international trade compliance platform that helps exporters navigate complex regulatory requirements across multiple markets. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets, manual tracking, and scattered government websites with a single, intelligent dashboard — reducing compliance risk, saving time, and preventing costly shipment delays.

---

## Demo 1 — Dashboard Overview

The Compliance Dashboard is the central command center for all your trade operations.

| Feature | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Overall Risk Score** | A single at-a-glance metric showing your compliance standing across all markets and regulations. |
| **Critical Alerts** | Real-time notifications for regulatory changes that directly affect your shipments — CBAM deadlines, EUDR enforcement, Digital Product Passport requirements, and more. |
| **Tracked Markets** | Visual overview of every region you export to (EU, UAE, etc.) with per-market compliance status. |
| **Active FTAs** | Shows which Free Trade Agreements apply to your products and where duty savings are available. |
| **Live Alert Feed** | A rolling timeline of regulatory updates so your compliance team never misses a critical change. |

**Business Value:** Instead of monitoring dozens of government websites and regulatory feeds manually, ComplianceOS aggregates everything into one view — saving your team hours every week and eliminating the risk of missed deadlines.

## Demo 2 — Core Features Walkthrough

Five integrated modules power day-to-day compliance operations:

### 1. Risk Analysis
Select a target market and instantly see a breakdown of risk factors with actionable recommendations. ComplianceOS identifies what could go wrong *before* goods leave your facility.

### 2. Compliance Checklists
Interactive, regulation-specific checklists with a live progress bar. Your team can track exactly what has been completed and what is still outstanding for each shipment — no spreadsheets needed.

### 3. Regulatory Alerts
Deep-dive into active regulatory changes including:
- EU Digital Product Passport mandate
- CBAM carbon reporting requirements
- EUDR deforestation enforcement timelines

Each alert includes clear action items and deadlines.

### 4. FTA & Schemes Analysis
See which Free Trade Agreements and preferential schemes apply to your products. ComplianceOS calculates potential duty savings so you can make informed sourcing and routing decisions.

### 5. Shipment Tracking
Monitor active shipments with per-shipment compliance scores. Instantly see which shipments are cleared and which need attention before reaching customs.

**Business Value:** These five modules replace what typically requires separate tools, spreadsheets, and manual tracking. Everything your compliance team needs is in one place — structured, searchable, and always up to date.

## Demo 3 — Label Validator & Settings

### Label Validator (New Feature)

Incorrect product labeling is one of the top reasons shipments get held at customs. The Label Validator solves this.

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **Product & Country Selection** | Choose a product category (e.g., Food & Agriculture) and destination country to load the exact labeling rules that apply. |
| **Rule-by-Rule Validation** | Toggle individual labeling requirements and watch the compliance score update in real time (0% → 75% → 100%). |
| **Regulation References** | Expand any rule to see the specific regulation citation and compliance guidance. |
| **Country Switching** | Switch from EU to UAE and see requirements change instantly — Arabic-language labeling, Halal certification, and other region-specific rules appear automatically. |
| **Bulk Actions** | "Mark All Compliant" and "Reset All" for fast management of large rule sets. |

**Business Value:** Your team verifies label compliance *before* printing, avoiding costly delays, re-labeling, and rejected shipments at the border.
