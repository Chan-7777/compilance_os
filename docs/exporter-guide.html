⚠️ Compliance Risk

Know exactly where you stand in each market

Risk scores are calculated per country based on your product category, company size, and current regulations. Each score comes with a clear breakdown of contributing factors and actionable recommendations.

  • Per-country risk cards — individual score (0–100) with gauge visualisation
  • Risk Factors — each factor explained in plain English (e.g. "CBAM applies to steel exports")
  • Recommendations — specific steps to reduce your risk score
  • CBAM Readiness — for carbon-heavy products, estimate CO₂ emissions and track readiness
  • Scores are live-calculated from World Customs Org + EU Commission data

🔔 Regulatory Updates

Stay ahead of every regulatory change

Live regulatory alerts filtered to your exact product and target markets. No generic news — only changes that directly affect your exports, explained in plain language.

  • Three severity levels — Urgent (act now), Review Soon (within 30 days), FYI (awareness)
  • Search — full-text search across all alert messages and countries
  • Filter by severity with one click
  • Plain-language impact — each alert explains what it means specifically for you
  • Alert types — Deadlines, Regulation Changes, FTA Updates, Tariff Changes
  • Deadline countdown — deadline alerts show a live badge (e.g. "14 days left", "2d overdue") coloured red when urgent
  • Dismiss & restore — dismiss individual alerts with ✕, or use "Mark all read". Dismissed alerts are saved and can be shown/restored at any time
  • Expandable detail per alert showing type, market, and exact date
  • Sources: EU Commission, India DGFT, World Customs Organization

🌐 Trade Deals & Savings PRO

Maximise savings through FTAs and export schemes

India has active trade deals — are you using them? This module shows every applicable Free Trade Agreement and export scheme, and calculates exactly how much you save per shipment.

  • FTA Agreement Browser — all applicable agreements with status (Active / Under Negotiation)
  • Export Schemes — RoDTEP, MEIS, AEO, and other incentive schemes
  • Tariff Savings Calculator — enter shipment value, see MFN vs preferential duty and exact savings in ₹
  • Compare MFN rate vs preferential rate side by side
  • Covers: UAE (CEPA active), Japan, Australia, EU (in negotiation)

🚚 Shipments PRO

Track every shipment from preparation to delivery

Full shipment lifecycle management with AI-powered compliance gating. Every shipment is checked against current regulations before it leaves your facility.

  • Add shipments — name, product, destination, date, HS code, value
  • Auto-save draft — form is saved automatically as you type. If you close or refresh, your in-progress shipment is restored with a "Draft saved" notice. Discard any time.
  • Document OCR — drag-and-drop invoice upload, AI auto-extracts all fields (OpenAI gpt-4o)
  • Pre-Shipment Compliance Gate — run automated check: Approved / Blocked / Needs Review
  • HS Code Classifier — AI classifies your product by commodity code
  • Landed Cost Calculator — total import cost including duties and freight
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) — auto-generate PDF for FTA eligibility
  • Deal Pack — multi-country export document bundle in one click
  • TReDS Financing — submit for trade receivables discounting
  • Customs Payload — auto-generate customs declaration data

  • Interactive Q&A checklist — tick off each requirement with expandable guidance
  • Real-time compliance score (0–100%) — green / orange / red with status badge
  • AI Label Vision Scanner — upload a photo of your actual label, AI auto-checks all rules
  • Bulk actions — Mark All Compliant or Reset All
  • Auto-expands failed rules so you focus on what needs fixing

⚙️ Settings

Configure your compliance profile

Everything personalised to your company — set once, used everywhere.

🏢

Profile & Markets

Your company's core identity — drives all risk scores, checklists and alerts.

  • Company name, size (Micro / Small / Medium / Large)
  • IEC (Import-Export Code) — India DGFT identifier
  • GSTIN — GST identification number for tax documentation
  • Product category — determines applicable regulations
  • Target markets — EU, US, UK, UAE, Japan, Australia
  • Selecting EU unlocks the EU Compliance module in sidebar
  • Customs Filing Details — company address, city, state, PIN, state code, and port of loading (used to auto-fill ICEGATE customs declarations)
