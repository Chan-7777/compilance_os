The Problem We Solve Together

Indian MSME exporters lose crores every year to a problem that isn't lack of orders or lack of finance — it's friction across the trade cycle. Documentation errors cause 70% of shipping delays. India's FTA utilization rate is just 25%. CBAM's definitive phase is now live, and most Indian steel and aluminium exporters have no emissions data.

No single player solves this end-to-end. Compliance consultants don't do finance. Lenders don't do compliance. Freight forwarders do neither. The exporter coordinates between 5 different parties for a single shipment.

Our thesis: Compliance + Finance as a single integrated flow — not separate silos. ComplianceOS provides the intelligence layer. BEN Finserv provides the finance execution layer. Together, we offer something nobody else in India offers: a compliance-gated, finance-ready export workflow.

How It Works

Benedict's Free Compliance Review form collects the exporter's product category, HS codes, destination markets, turnover, and compliance awareness. Today, this data goes to a Google Sheet and Benedict calls them manually.

With ComplianceOS integrated, the moment an exporter submits the form, they receive an instant compliance risk report — real numbers, not generic advice. Benedict gets a pre-qualified lead with full compliance context.

Protection Clauses

NDA required before pilot starts. These clauses protect both parties.

  • Mutual Confidentiality: Neither party discloses the other's business model, pricing, client data, technology architecture, or partnership terms to any third party.
  • Non-Replication: Benedict agrees not to build, commission, or acquire a competing compliance technology platform during the partnership and for 12 months after termination.
  • Data Protection: Exporter data collected through the joint funnel belongs to both parties. Neither can sell, share, or monetize independently without consent.
  • Termination: Either party can exit with 30 days written notice. On termination: API access is revoked, co-branded materials retired, exporter ComplianceOS accounts remain active, client relationships remain with Benedict.
  • Non-Compete: During partnership and 12 months after, neither party partners with a direct competitor for the same market segment.
