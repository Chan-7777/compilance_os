2. Frontend Design: Psychology & Persuasion

Applying the marketing-psychology and page-cro frameworks to the ComplianceOS frontend component design.

Hick's Law page-cro

Decision time increases with the number and complexity of choices.

Application in ComplianceOS:

Never show more than one Primary Call To Action per tracking screen. When evaluating an HS code risk, the user should only see one clear path forward.

Loss Aversion psychology

Losses feel roughly twice as painful as equivalent gains feel good.

Application in ComplianceOS:

When alerting an MSME about an upcoming EU CBAM requirement, frame it around the liability they will suffer rather than the "smooth border entry" they will gain.

Shipment At Risk of Seizure

Avoid impound fees at the port of entry by filing the CBAM declaration now.

Zeigarnik Effect onboarding-cro

Unfinished tasks occupy the mind more than completed ones. Open loops create tension.

Application in ComplianceOS:

Use progress bars everywhere. The "Shipment Readiness" score natively abuses this effect to encourage the user to upload all their commercial invoices.

3. B2B Trust & Social Proof

Deploying the marketing-psychology Authority Bias and Bandwagon Effect inside the app interface.

The "Zonos Landed Cost" Banner

Using Authority Bias. By explicitly stating that our duties and taxes calculations are powered by a recognized global leader, we pass liability off the exporter.

100% Guaranteed Landed Cost

Powered by Zonos. Your margins are fully protected against customs discrepancy. Any charges above this quoted amount will be completely absorbed.

Rule of 100 (FTA Savings)

Using the Rule of 100 logic: Since B2B shipments are high ticket (over $100), absolute numbers feel much larger than percentages.
