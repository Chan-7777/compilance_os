Growth Marketing Playbook

A comprehensive Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy leveraging 29 mental models and frameworks from the coreyhaines31/marketingskills repository.

01

Strategy & Monetization

pricing-strategy marketing-psychology launch-strategy marketing-ideas

ComplianceOS solves a highly complex B2B problem (global trade compliance and liability transfer). Our strategy leans on high-trust psychological models.

  • Pricing Strategy (Decoy Effect): We will use the Decoy Effect in our pricing tiers.
    • Starter: $299/mo (Self-serve)
    • Professional: $499/mo (Includes CBAM & Zonos integrations) — Target Tier
    • Enterprise: Custom pricing (Decoy to make $499 look affordable)
  • Marketing Psychology (Authority & Loss Aversion): Exporters care more about not losing a shipment to customs impound than gaining a slight efficiency. All messaging must frame ComplianceOS as "loss prevention" (Liability Transfer via Zonos) rather than "workflow enhancement."
  • Launch Strategy: Launch exclusively targeting one vertical first (e.g., Apparels & Textiles to the EU). Depth before breadth (Critical Mass Tipping Point).
02

Growth Engineering

free-tool-strategy referral-program

Code can be our best marketer. We will build free tools to generate inbound MQLs (Marketing Qualified Leads).

  • Free Tool Strategy (Reciprocity Principle):
    • HS Code Classifier Toolkit: Extract the zonos-classify Edge Function into an un-gated mini-site (e.g., hscodelookup.complianceos.com). Users get 5 free natural-language lookups before hitting a paywall.
    • EU CBAM Calculator: Extract the climatiq-emissions Edge Function to let manufacturers calculate their Scope 3 emissions for free.
  • Referral Program: Double-sided rewards with B2B value. When a manufacturer refers their supplier, the supplier gets 1 month free, and the manufacturer gets an elevated API rate limit for document OCR processing.
03

SEO & Discovery

programmatic-seo ai-seo competitor-alternatives schema-markup seo-audit
  • Programmatic SEO: ComplianceOS operates in a data-rich environment. We will generate thousands of programmatic landing pages based on trade corridors and product categories.
    • URL Template: complianceos.com/export-[PRODUCT-CATEGORY]-from-india-to-[COUNTRY-CODE]
    • Example: complianceos.com/export-steel-from-india-to-eu-cbam-requirements
  • Competitor Alternatives: Create high-converting Comparison Pages vs. Legacy Forwarders (e.g., "ComplianceOS vs. Flexport Customs", "ComplianceOS vs. Avalara").
  • AI SEO (AEO/GEO): Optimize content specifically for LLM extraction (ChatGPT, Perplexity). Structure our regulatory database in clean tables and bullet points so AI engines cite ComplianceOS as the source of truth for "How to export X."
04

Content & Copywriting

cold-email email-sequence social-content copywriting copy-editing
  • B2B Cold Email (Foot-in-the-Door): Target Supply Chain Directors and Export Managers.
    Hook: "Saw you're exporting [Category] to the EU. Are you fully prepared for the new CBAM reporting requirements hitting this quarter?"

    Offer: Don't pitch the software. Pitch a free "CBAM Readiness Audit" (using our Free Tool).
  • Email Sequence (Zeigarnik Effect): Abandoned cart/onboarding sequences. "You're 80% done with your cross-border compliance setup. Complete your company profile to unlock Zonos Landed Cost Guarantees."
  • Social Content: LinkedIn is the primary distribution channel for trade compliance. Post teardowns of recent trade fines, customs horror stories, and regulatory changes (Regulatory Alerts feature).
05

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

page-cro signup-flow-cro onboarding-cro form-cro popup-cro paywall-upgrade-cro
  • Page CRO (Hick’s Law): The homepage must have a single compelling CTA. Remove complex navigation.
    • Hero Headline: "Guaranteed Landed Costs & Zero-Touch Customs. For Modern Exporters."
    • CTA: "Scan Your First Shipment — Free"
  • Signup Flow CRO (Endowment Effect): Do not ask for arbitrary data upfront. Use the document-ocr feature as the top-of-funnel! Allow users to drag-and-drop a Bill of Lading on the homepage. The system extracts it, formats a beautiful dashboard, and then asks them to create an account to save their completed assessment.
  • Paywall Upgrade CRO: Gate premium trust features. The Zonos "100% Guaranteed Landed Cost" badge acts as an innate upgrade driver. MSMEs can calculate duties on the free plan, but to get the Guarantee Shield, they must upgrade.
06

Paid & Distribution

paid-ads ad-creative
  • Paid Ads (LinkedIn & Search): Focus on high-intent Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU) search queries.
    • Keywords: "automate ICEGATE filing," "CBAM emissions reporting software," "determine HS code API."
  • Ad Creative (Pratfall Effect & Contrast Effect): Use the "Before & After" Contrast framework for ad creatives.
    • Before: A massive stack of disorganized PDFs and government spreadsheets.
    • After: The clean, single-click "Generate Filing Payload" JSON screen from ComplianceOS.
07

Measurement & Testing

analytics-tracking ab-test-setup
  • Analytics Tracking (North Star Metric): Our North Star metric is Total Shipment Value Processed DDP. This proves clients actually trust the system with financial liability.
    • Event Tracking: We need precise PostHog/Mixpanel funnels tracking the drop-off rate between: Document Uploaded -> HS Code Validated -> Landed Cost Calculated -> Customs Payload Generated.
  • A/B Test Setup: Continuously refine the friction points.
    • Test Hypothesis: Does showing the exact dollar value of "FTA Savings" (Rule of 100) before checkout increase the conversion rate to the paid tier vs. showing a generic "Unlock Savings" banner?
08

Retention

churn-prevention
  • Churn Prevention (Sunk Cost Fallacy & Switching Costs): Increase the switching costs ethically. The more a company uses ComplianceOS to store their vendor network (via the WhatsApp outreach integration for Scope 3 data) and their product catalogs (with cached HS codes), the harder it becomes to leave the platform.
  • Offboarding Flow: If a user clicks cancel, trigger a dynamic save offer offering a temporary downgrade to a "data-storage only" tier so they don't lose their historical shipment compliance records.
